                                          YOUR WRITING WORKSPACE

                                          Welcome to TaleTutorial.

                                          Choose a quick start or a guide to each area. Your work is saved in this browser on this device.

                                          Quick start

                                          1. Create a character. In Studio, choose New character, add a name, and save. Add more details whenever you’re ready.
                                          2. Start writing. Open Stories, choose New story, and start typing. Your draft saves automatically on this device.
                                          3. Build your world. Add locations in Places. Use World for groups, artifacts, magic systems, species, languages, religions, and glossary terms.
                                          4. Find things quickly. Use the search at the top to find characters, stories, places, and world entries. Change the app’s appearance in Settings → Appearance.
                                          5. Back up your work. Watch the Studio header for your save and backup status. Open it to reach Settings → Data & Storage, export a full backup, or import one. You can also export stories as Markdown, plain text, Word, or EPUB, and print profiles or story previews to PDF.

                                          Your work is stored in this browser, not in the cloud. Clearing this browser’s site data, switching browser profiles, or losing the device can remove it. Export a full backup and keep it somewhere separate.

                                          Detailed guide

                                          01

                                          Create characters

                                          Start with a name, then add personality, background, and other details. Optional sections include a character questionnaire, dialogue samples, goals, fears, and an arc. Use Character arc & snapshots to track how a character changes. Add connections to describe how characters relate, or compare two characters in Cast.

                                          02

                                          Write stories

                                          Create a story from scratch or start with a template. The compact formatting toolbar can stay out of your way, and Focus mode leaves just your title and writing area. Organize chapters in the sidebar, see chapter and story word counts, and add chapter-specific notes. The selected chapter shows the previous chapter’s summary; the continuity checker flags possible age, chronology, location, availability, and cast-link conflicts from your snapshots and mentions. Checks are suggestions, not edits. Quick peek can collapse or resize, and Version history shows timestamps, lets you compare two saved versions, or restore one. The Project Dashboard links your story to its cast, places, timeline events, continuity review, and Reading Mode.

                                          03

                                          Organize a book series

                                          Create a series, then open each book’s details and choose that series and book number. Add shared characters and places, and record important events on the series timeline. Series data is included in full backups.

                                          04

                                          Plan and track your writing

                                          Use the Dashboard to see a story’s word count, chapters, linked characters, progress, and unfinished tasks. In the editor, set a daily word goal, use Focus mode, and add notes or tasks. The Plan area includes outlines, timelines, relationship maps, and prompts. Use Relationship History to track a connection’s status by chapter; snapshots with the same chapter or timeline label appear beside each change.

                                          05

                                          Build your world

                                          Use Places to describe locations. Use World for factions, artifacts, magic or technology systems, species, languages, religions, and glossary entries. Add tags to organize your work, then search across the workspace.

                                          06

                                          Save and back up your work

                                          Your work is saved in this browser on this device. The Studio header shows save and backup status; open it to reach Settings → Data & Storage, export a full backup, or restore one. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles, or losing the device can remove your writing. Keep a backup somewhere separate.

                                          07

                                          Appearance and offline use

                                          Choose a light or dark theme in Settings → Appearance. When opened from a secure website or a local server, Fiction Tale can be installed and used offline. Your writing stays in this browser, even when you’re offline. Some optional fonts may not load without an internet connection.

                                          08

                                          Draft conversations

                                          Use Dialogue to write a conversation separately from your story. Choose participating characters, add emotional context, then add the conversation to a story when it’s ready.

                                          Keep your work safe: your writing is not backed up online. Export a full backup regularly and keep a copy somewhere separate from this browser and device.
