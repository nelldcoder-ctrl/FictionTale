                              YOUR WRITING WORKSPACE

                              Welcome to TaleTutorial.

                              Choose the quick tour or explore each tool in detail. Your work is saved in this browser on this device.

                              Simple tutorial

                              1. Create a character. Choose New character, add a name, then save. The profile and preview grow with your ideas.
                              2. Start writing. Open Stories, choose New story, and begin. Drafts autosave on this device.
                              3. Build your world. Add a place or a World Entry such as a faction, artifact, magic system, species, language, religion, or glossary term.
                              4. Find things quickly. Use the search at the top to find characters, stories, places, and world entries. Switch themes in Settings under Appearance.
                              5. Keep a backup. Open Settings → Data & Storage to export a full backup or import one. Export stories as Markdown, text, Word, or EPUB; print to PDF or save a character-sheet image when you want a shareable copy.

                              Your work stays in this browser unless you export it or connect a backup folder. Browser data can be cleared, so keep a separate backup.

                              Detail tutorial

                              01

                              Characters that grow

                              Create a profile, then explore the questionnaire, dialogue samples, and completeness suggestions. In Character arc & snapshots, record the starting belief, turning point, truth learned, and chapter-by-chapter appearance, knowledge, and trust. Connections can have a type, status, history, and status changes over time. Compare two characters side by side from Character Cards.

                              02

                              Stories and continuity

                              Open a story to draft, organize chapters, assign characters and places, and use @Name mentions to link characters. Character profiles show their story appearances. Add a place to a chapter to check continuity against character locations, absences, and deaths; record what a character knows in timeline events. Use Quick peek to look up characters, places, and world entries without leaving the editor.

                              03

                              Writing flow and planning

                              Set a daily word goal and track your streak. Focus mode hides distractions; chapter stats show pacing and reading time. Keep story notes and to-dos, and link planning scene cards to story chapters. The Plan area also includes relationship maps, a shared chronology, prompts, and outline tools.

                              04

                              Places and worldbuilding

                              Use Places for settings, then use World for factions, artifacts, magic or technology systems, species, languages, religions, and glossary entries. Add tags to organize characters, stories, places, and world entries, then search across the whole workspace.

                              05

                              Save, export, and share

                              Full-data backups contain your library and planning data; export or import them as JSON or YAML from Settings under Data & Storage. The story export menu offers Markdown, plain text, Word (.docx), and EPUB. Print profiles or story previews to PDF, or export a text-based character-sheet image. Choose a backup folder for an additional local copy.

                              06

                              Theme, install, and offline use

                              Switch between light and dark themes in Settings under Appearance. When served from localhost or HTTPS, Fiction Tale can be installed and caches its app shell for offline access. Large images, drafts, and version history are stored in IndexedDB to reduce localStorage pressure. External fonts may fall back while offline; your writing remains stored locally in the browser.

                              Protect your work: browser storage is local, not cloud backup. Export a full backup from Settings occasionally and keep it somewhere separate from this browser.
