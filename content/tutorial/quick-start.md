1. **Choose a project.** A project keeps its stories, characters, places, and plans together. Try an editable sample project to see how they connect, or create your own.
2. **Write a story.** Open **Writing → Stories**, choose **New story**, and start typing. Your draft saves on this device. Add chapters or notes whenever they help.
3. **Organize your cast.** Start each character with a name and short description. Put them in a **Cast Group** such as Main or Supporting; use tags for details like “Mage” or “Merchant.”
4. **Build your setting.** Use **Worldbuilding → Places** for locations. Use **World Entries** for things such as groups, objects, magic, species, languages, or terms you want to remember.
5. **Find your next step.** Use a prompt or scene card to get an idea moving. Search from the top of the workspace to find characters, stories, places, or world entries.
6. **Keep a backup.** Check the workspace header for save and backup status. Open it to reach **Settings → Data & Storage** and export a full backup. New backup imports become separate projects; replacing matching older data asks for confirmation. You can also download stories as Markdown, plain text, Word (.docx), or EPUB, and print profiles or story previews to PDF.

> Your work is stored in this browser, not in the cloud. Clearing this browser’s site data, switching browser profiles, or losing the device can remove it. Export a full backup and keep it somewhere separate.
